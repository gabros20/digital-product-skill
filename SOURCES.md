Coordination-mode shapes (the one-page brief, the dense report, artifacts-on-disk,
ledger-before-memory) are distilled from the author's separate `orchestrate` skill's practice;
`orchestrate` is not part of this family, is not required by this pack, and none of its reference
content is reproduced here.

No external literature is cited by this pack in its own voice. Where a specialist pack grounds a
seam in external evidence, the citation lives in that pack and its own SOURCES.md — this registry
points at the pack, never past it.

What this pack claims for itself: the stage taxonomy's naming (Define/Design/Shape/Build/Assure/
Run/Improve/Distribute), the minimal-path examples, the LEDGER.md format, and the
escalation-on-need mode ladder are this pack's own synthesis (2026-08-03/04).
